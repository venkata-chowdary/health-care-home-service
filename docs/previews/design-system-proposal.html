Reference analysis

What the reference does, and what we change.

The reference is a calm, centred, single-column site: white space does the work, headings are tight and medium-weight, and one electric blue carries every action.

ElementKeepChange for the hospital
TypeOne geometric grotesk, medium-weight headings with tight tracking, sentences that end in full stops.Body text in the reference is 12–13px light grey. We go to 16px at 5.9:1 contrast so older visitors can read it.
ColourWhite canvas, deep navy for text and one feature panel, electric blue for actions, pale blue tint for eyebrows and highlight cards.Blue is used as text only in a darker shade (#1757D6) so links pass AA. Red is reserved for urgent-care messaging.
HeaderLogo left, five quiet links centred, blue pill CTA right.Add the phone number beside the CTA. Calling is a primary goal and the reference hides it in the footer.
HeroCentred eyebrow pill, two-sentence headline, two buttons, a bleeding strip of rounded photos with floating tick badges, a four-up fact row.Second button becomes "Call" rather than "Explore". Badges and facts only state things the client has confirmed.
CardsHairline-bordered white cards at 16px radius, small tinted icon tile, "Learn more →" link, one tinted feature card in a bento grid.Bento collapses to two columns on tablet and one on mobile with the feature card first.
PhotographyBright, clinical, natural-light photos of real staff, cropped 4:5 for people.Real hospital photography only. Placeholders stay visibly labelled until the client supplies photos.
Feature panelInset navy panel at 24px radius with photo left and label/value rows right.Used for Facilities, with every row marked provisional until confirmed.
Visit sectionWide photo with a floating white card: address, hours table, full-width directions button. Two tinted info cards below.The card adds a tap-to-call line and an open/closed status. On mobile the card drops below the photo instead of covering it.
FAQCentred heading, two-column grid of separate bordered items with a round plus.Answers that need clinical or operational sign-off carry a visible tag in the content file.
GapsThe reference has no reviews, no form and no mobile view.Review cards, the enquiry form and the mobile layouts below are new, built in the same language.
